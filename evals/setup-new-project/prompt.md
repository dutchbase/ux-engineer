I cannot answer questions during this session, so here are my answers up front. If something important is still unknown, record it as an open question instead of asking me.

- Product: Shelfshare, a tool-lending web app for neighbours. Locale en-GB.
- Customers: two types. Tool owners (often 40+, own many tools, want them back in good condition) and borrowers (often renters in flats, need a tool once for a small job, want it fast and free).
- Borrowers mostly use their phone, standing in the hallway or at the shop. Owners use phone and laptop.
- Biggest frustrations: borrowers do not know if a tool is really available; owners worry about damage and late returns.
- People leave when the pickup arrangement takes too many messages.
- We have no user research or analytics yet. I know this from talking to about ten neighbours myself.
- Tone: friendly, plain, a bit neighbourly. Avoid corporate words like "solution" and "leverage".
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Screen sizes: the defaults are fine.
- Design system: none yet.
- Key flows: list a tool, request a tool, agree on a pickup time.
- No web research needed.
