## 5. Interview guide

- Opening (2 minutes): purpose, no right answers, permission to stop.
- Context questions: how the person does this task today.
- Recent-event questions: "Tell me about the last time you ..."
- Task section: see section 4. Ask the participant to think aloud.
- Closing: anything we missed, who else to ask.
- Neutral probes: "Say more about that." "What did you expect?"
- Avoid: leading questions, yes/no questions, asking people to predict.
